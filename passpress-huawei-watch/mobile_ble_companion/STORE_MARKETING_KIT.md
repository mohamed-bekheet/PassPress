### **Full Description:**

Tired of typing complex, 30-character passwords on your PC or laptop? Meet **PassPress** — the ultimate hardware-backed password transmitter that turns your Android phone into a virtual Bluetooth LE Keyboard!

With PassPress, your phone acts as a physical keyboard for your PC, Mac, Linux, or Tablet. Simply tap a slot or a home-screen widget, scan your fingerprint, and watch your password automatically type out into any login field.

#### ✨ Key Features:

- ⌨️ **Standard Bluetooth LE Keyboard:** No software or driver installation needed on your PC/Mac! Connects natively just like a wireless keyboard or mouse.
- 🛡️ **Hardware Security & Encryption:** Passwords are stored in Android’s hardware-backed Keystore using AES-256 encryption.
- 🚫 **Zero Internet Needed:** PassPress does not request or use the Internet permission. Your vault never leaves your device.
- 👆 **One-Tap Home Screen Widgets:** Send passwords instantly right from your phone's launcher without even opening the app.
- 🔒 **Biometric Security & Per-Device Trust:** Authorize trusted PCs to auto-type, or require fingerprint verification for untrusted devices.
- 📥 **Encrypted Backup & Restore:** Export your entire vault into an AES-256-GCM encrypted backup file protected by your own Master Password.
- ⚡ **Android Quick Settings Tile:** Connect and disconnect your virtual keyboard directly from your Android notification shade.
- ⏱️ **Anti-Paste Detection Bypass:** Adjust keystroke delay sliders to bypass websites and games that block clipboard pasting.
