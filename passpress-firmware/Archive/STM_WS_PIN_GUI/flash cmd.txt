STM32_Programmer_CLI.exe -c port=USB1 -w "build\Release\STM_WS.elf" -v
STM32_Programmer_CLI.exe -c port=USB1 -w "build\Release\STM_WS.elf" -v -rst
STM32_Programmer_CLI.exe -c port=USB1 -ob RDP=0x00

Examples:
-c port=SWD freq=4000 → connect via ST-LINK SWD at 4 MHz
-c port=USB1 → connect via USB DFU bootloader
-c port=COM3 br=115200 → connect via UART bootloader
-w ... -v -rst = write, verify, then reset.
